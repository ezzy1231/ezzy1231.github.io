Motion

Ads and product films, built in code

I direct and animate 3D and 2D pieces in three.js, WebGL shaders and Canvas, from the first beat sheet to the final mix. I built my own AI pipeline for it: every frame is rendered from a timeline I can check shot by shot, voice-overs come from ElevenLabs and local TTS models, and sound is real recorded foley mixed to broadcast loudness. Recent work includes a 4K Amharic ad for an AI tutoring app and a narrated explainer timed word by word to its voice.

Software

Real products, from database to UI

I've spent about two years shipping TypeScript and JavaScript products end to end: real-time systems, background job queues, Postgres schemas, Telegram bots and AI voice features. I built and run HabeshaTTS, an Amharic voice-donation platform for streamers, and UrbanExplore, a booking marketplace for Addis Ababa with security enforced in the database.
